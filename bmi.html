UNDERSTANDING YOUR SCORE

What is BMI?

Body Mass Index (BMI) is a clinical measurement of body composition based on your height and weight. It serves as an essential benchmark for setting realistic body transformation targets.

Our certified coaches at India Fitness use BMI along with body fat percentage to create custom nutrition and workout regimens tailored to your metabolic rate.

BMI Range Category
Less than 18.5 Underweight
18.5 – 24.9 Normal Weight
25.0 – 29.9 Overweight
30.0 or Higher Obese
